RULES FOR THE MOTIF

  1. A cell always means a result. Never decorate with it.
  2. Time runs left to right, oldest to newest.
  3. Rows are hosts or templates; columns are tasks or time.
  4. Every cell is clickable and lands on the output.
  5. Lime is reserved for live and for the one primary action.
  6. Mono for names the machine gave; sans for words people wrote.
